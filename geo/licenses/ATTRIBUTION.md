# Geography attribution

- Natural Earth 5.1.2, 1:50m countries and 1:110m populated places. Public domain. [Terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/), [source](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2).
- NASA Earth Observatory: Blue Marble: Next Generation – Base Map, July 2004. Produced by Reto Stöckli (NASA Goddard Space Flight Center). [Source download](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/), [credits and processing](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/). NASA requests credit “NASA Earth Observatory” when using or republishing this dataset; it is displayed on the globe. The 21600×10800 global JPEG (2km/pixel) is subdivided into 675px geographic XYZ tiles (levels 0–4), lower levels are LANCZOS downsampled, and JPEGs use quality 90 / 4:4:4. Source checksum and output checksums are versioned. No separate cloud layer is included; surface snow and ice remain.
- geoBoundaries gbOpen, South Korea ADM1: Natural Earth, Public Domain. ADM2: geoBoundaries/citypopulation.de, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Original license, boundary ID, immutable source URL and represented year are recorded per dataset in `geo/sources.json` and in the public manifest. [Dataset and attribution](https://www.geoboundaries.org/), [API](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api.html), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Geometry is simplified for globe rendering. Administrative IDs use `boundaryID:shapeID`, not national official codes. ADM2 parents are derived from at least 80% polygon overlap with the selected ADM1 dataset; ambiguous relationships are omitted from the bundle and documented in the manifest. Coordinates are original unsimplified polygon centroids or Natural Earth capital points, used as presentation fallbacks.

Country boundaries use 0.01-degree simplification. Label anchors are polygon interior representative points, separate from news coordinates. Runtime overview uses the bundled raster; close views use streamed imagery with local vector polygons and borders.

- OpenStreetMap standard tiles: © OpenStreetMap contributors, [copyright / ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/). Close views request visible tiles directly in the browser; no tiles are bundled in this repository. Optional Cesium ion imagery uses the account token and provider credits supplied by Cesium.
